Adaptation for this portfolio: selected cortex, cerebellum, and brainstem
meshes; decoded and combined geometry; centered and uniformly normalized
vertex coordinates; converted to a compact indexed binary format, gzip-compressed and wrapped in a local JavaScript asset.
The adapted geometry in brain-anatomy.js remains under CC BY-SA 4.0.
Full upstream license notices: brain-source-license.txt.

The scan and highlighted region are conceptual overlays for the portfolio,
not a patient MRI, clinical segmentation, or medical diagnosis.
