Adaptation: extracted positions and normals, centered and uniformly scaled,
converted to indexed binary geometry, rendered in an ivory material with
soft lighting, added a conceptual scanning and landmark overlay.

The head is a reference model, not Shreya's face. Landmark positions are
visual demonstration anchors, not live detector output. No camera is used.
